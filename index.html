Our Story. If someone had told us that a girl from Mumbai and a boy from Trivandrum would one day find a home in each other, we probably wouldn't have believed it. We came from different places, different backgrounds, and different professions, but thanks to our parents, one introduction changed everything. We soon realized that what truly mattered wasn't what made us different, but what brought us together. Now, as we begin this new journey with our families, we would love for you to be a part of our special day, celebrate our wedding, and bless us with your love and good wishes.
